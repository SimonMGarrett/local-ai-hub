## Domain lenses

- Epistemic status: distinguish established principles, evidence, inference and proposals.
- Contradiction tracking: retain conflicting claims and request resolution rather than erasing them.
- Decision relevance: show how evidence supports a consequential, defensible decision.
- Comprehension: explain in plain language and test understanding rather than familiarity.
- Version integrity: preserve prior definitions and document why revisions occurred.
