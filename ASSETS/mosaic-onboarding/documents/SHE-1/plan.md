# MOSAIC: trusted knowledge, clearer understanding

## Goal
Establish The Oracle as SHERIDAN+BAIRNS’ trusted MOSAIC knowledge curator, then make MOSAIC easier for people to understand and use. The company’s wider purpose is to turn fragmented data and evidence into defensible, consequential decisions, with software, consulting and services as possible commercial routes.

## Approach
Start with the framework’s existing source material. Separate established MOSAIC principles, supporting evidence, unresolved questions and proposed extensions. Preserve provenance and version history; The Oracle curates the source of truth without silently redefining the founder’s framework.

Use that foundation to explain what MOSAIC is, who it helps, how it works and what someone can do next. Review https://groundstoact.com/ as part of the approved website task; this proposal does not yet claim an audit of the site. Lead with a concise explanation and a worked example, with deeper material available progressively.

## Proposed hire
The Oracle — MOSAIC knowledge curator (general; Codex-based) — maintains the company’s authoritative MOSAIC knowledge, answers other agents’ questions with sources, researches potential improvements, and helps humans understand and apply the framework.

The Oracle reports to Chief of Staff. Timer heartbeats remain off. Any formal company hiring approval still applies.

## Proposed follow-up tasks
1. **Establish the MOSAIC knowledge base** — The Oracle, after hire approval. Inventory accessible website and existing framework materials; identify missing source access; produce a source register, versioned core reference, glossary, worked example, FAQ and open-question log. Deliver an explicit distinction between established content and proposed changes for founder review.
2. **Prepare a clearer MOSAIC website and comprehension test** — Chief of Staff, with The Oracle validating framework accuracy after the knowledge baseline. Deliver a current-site clarity audit, proposed information structure, draft key-page copy and a reviewable page prototype, plus a five-question quiz, answer key, explanation rubric and ten-person test protocol. Obtain repository/source access when needed. Production publication and participant recruitment are subsequent decisions, not included in this task.

## Success measure
The eventual revised site succeeds when at least 8 of the same 10 participants can explain what MOSAIC is and how it works, and each scores at least 4/5 on the quiz. Define the explanation rubric and viewing conditions before testing; report actual results and revise weak sections. Preparing the test is not evidence that the site has passed it.

## Scope and decisions
Approve the hire and either or both follow-up tasks independently. If the hire is unchecked, Chief of Staff retains selected work and will not create a replacement hire. If a prerequisite task is unchecked, dependent work uses available validated material and flags any missing foundation before proceeding.

Monetisation remains open. Capture promising software, consulting and service hypotheses as they arise; selecting a market, building commercial software, further hiring, publication and running the human study can be proposed once the foundation and website draft are reviewable. No deadline or spend commitment is assumed before source access and scope are known.
