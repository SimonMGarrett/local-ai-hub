## Wording finding: “stages”
ConfidenceAndCertaintyPage.tsx:65 uses “Its stages”. Core C7 itself allows numbered stages while explicitly rejecting a one-way pipeline. The article promises no formula for certainty and describes later challenge/reconsideration. Thus “stages” alone is not a material contradiction.

Suggested clarity change for Chief of Staff to present with founder review, not silently apply during extraction: replace “Its stages help distinguish” with “Its six forms of reasoning, which can be revisited as needed, help distinguish”. This aligns the standalone article with the homepage and C7. Retaining the original is acceptable for the faithful extraction draft; do not describe a fixed six-step method elsewhere. Founder intent needs escalation only if a mandatory sequence is proposed.

## Concrete interpretive check — not a new website teaching example
Assume a team is choosing between a small reversible service trial and immediate full rollout, has incomplete feedback, owns the decision and can review trial results. The articles support recording feedback origins, an alternative explanation and what evidence would change that choice (C1–C6/C8–C10), then recording residual uncertainty and a review condition (C6/C12). They do not establish which option wins, a confidence percentage, an empirical success rate or an automatic threshold. This hypothetical checks claim boundaries only; it is not an endorsed MOSAIC example and is not proposed for insertion into the site.
