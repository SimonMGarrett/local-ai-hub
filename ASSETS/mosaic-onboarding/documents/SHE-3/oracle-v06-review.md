## Concrete interpretive check
Curatorial hypothetical only, not an endorsed teaching example or proposed website insertion. Assume a service owner must choose a reversible two-week trial or immediate rollout, has incomplete feedback, and can revisit the decision. The bracketed forms permit Analysis to reveal that two feedback reports share one origin, returning to Observations/Structure (C2–C4/C7–C8). The record can retain that dependency, the rollout alternative, residual uncertainty and a review condition (C6/C12). Neither equal-sized cards nor rust labels establish equal evidential weight, a confidence percentage, or which choice wins. This checks the visual implications; it supplies no empirical result.
