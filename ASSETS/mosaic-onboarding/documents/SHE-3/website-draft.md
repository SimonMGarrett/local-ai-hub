## Key-page copy
### Home
**Decision framework**

# Make strong decisions from fragmented evidence

MOSAIC helps you make the most reasoned and defensible choice available when evidence is incomplete, uncertain, scattered, or open to more than one interpretation.

### What is MOSAIC?
MOSAIC is a framework for people making weighty decisions with incomplete, scattered or conflicting information. Use it to work towards the best-supported choice you can make with the information you have, and keep a reasoned audit trail of how you reached it. You remain responsible for the decision.

**Who is it for?** People facing choices with significant consequences, who want to examine their reasons carefully and be able to explain them later. The starting point is a decision that matters to you.

**What do you produce?** A reasoned audit trail: what you knew at the time, where it came from, assumptions you made, options you considered, why you chose, what remained uncertain and what would make you reconsider. If things go wrong, this record lets you revisit the reasoning in its original context. It does not guarantee a good outcome.

Primary action: Start with your decision. Secondary: See the six forms of reasoning.

### How it works
Use six forms of reasoning, revisiting them as gaps emerge. They are not a fixed one-way sequence.

- **Meaning:** What decision are we making? Name the owner, options, desired outcome, deadline and constraints. Produce a clear decision statement.
- **Observations:** What material do we have? Separate observations, reports, inferences and assumptions. Record origins and gaps.
- **Structure:** How does it connect? Link sources to claims and options. Preserve contradictions and shared origins.
- **Analysis:** What does the evidence support? Examine competing explanations and assumptions. Record findings and limits.
- **Interpretation:** What does that mean for our options? Consider consequences, risks and trade-offs, including uncertainty that could change the choice.
- **Choice:** What will we do, and why? Commit to the course best supported in context, and record the reasons, rejected alternatives, uncertainty and review conditions. Targeted inquiry may be warranted when its value justifies its cost.

### Start a decision
Write: “By [date], [owner] needs to choose between [options], to achieve [outcome], within [constraints].” Then list what you know, its sources and which uncertainty could change the choice. Use the six forms to examine it; retain reasons and review conditions with the choice. This prompt is an editorial aid, not another MOSAIC stage or a prescribed record format.

### About / FAQ
**Does MOSAIC decide for me?** No. Human judgement and responsibility remain.
**Does it guarantee a good outcome?** No. Preserve what was known at decision time and learn from later outcomes; a reasoned choice can still have an adverse outcome.
**Is it a score?** No canonical numerical scoring method is established here. Existing demonstration counts and weights are teaching devices.
**Why is Analysis also outside MOSAIC?** External analytical work supplies material. Internal Analysis examines structured evidence for this decision. Operational systems implement the chosen action.
**How established is it?** The public framework is developing. This draft explains a founder-confirmed website baseline, not measured effectiveness.
