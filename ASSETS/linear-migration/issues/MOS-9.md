# MOS-9 — Run a four-week zero-click discovery pilot for one validated MOSAIC insight

- Status: Backlog
- Project: none
- Updated: 2026-09-24T09:01:49.609Z

## Outcome

Test whether one validated MOSAIC insight can create understanding, recall and meaningful action when delivered natively on the two most credible discovery surfaces identified by <issue id="3c14dc44-f5f8-4f38-82e5-e3c1b6725e7e" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-8/audit-mosaics-zero-click-discovery-surfaces-using-reader-language">MOS-8</issue>.

## Why

The talk's useful strategic claim is that content must deliver value before a click and that last-click attribution cannot explain the full discovery journey. A short, bounded experiment can test this for MOSAIC without committing to a broad content programme.

## Entry criteria

Do not start until:

* <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue> has identified language readers understand and a useful first action;
* <issue id="3c14dc44-f5f8-4f38-82e5-e3c1b6725e7e" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-8/audit-mosaics-zero-click-discovery-surfaces-using-reader-language">MOS-8</issue> has identified no more than two credible surfaces;
* one worked example accurately demonstrates a MOSAIC principle;
* every proposed claim is traceable to source material;
* there is a meaningful, proportionate next action.

If any entry criterion fails, return the issue to Backlog with the missing evidence recorded.

## Scope

* Choose one validated worked example or proof unit.
* Define the internal client, job, audience, moment of use, desired action, source owner, success signal, distribution plan and refresh trigger.
* Create one reusable source brief and adapt it into native formats for no more than two channels.
* Publish one or two standalone value assets per week for four weeks.
* Keep founder and company roles distinct:
  * founder voice for perspective, judgement and lessons;
  * company surface for durable examples, methods and evidence.
* Use approximately five useful value deposits for each direct ask.
* When making an ask, describe the useful result and size of commitment.
* Record self-reported discovery when a meaningful action exists.
* Review learning weekly and evaluate patterns after the full pilot.

## Measurement

Track a simple sequence rather than claiming single-touch attribution:

1. **Audience:** relevant repeat readers, followers or subscribers.
2. **Reach:** qualified impressions, views or attention.
3. **Interest:** thoughtful replies, saves, shares, branded discovery or worked-example use.
4. **Outcome:** research participation, qualified conversations, repeat use, pilot interest or other validated action.

Compare weekly activity with meaningful actions over the following two to four weeks. Record qualitative evidence and confounders. Do not claim causality from correlation alone.

## Constraints

* Do not add more channels during the four-week pilot.
* Do not use mass-generated AI copy, synthetic engagement or forum seeding.
* Do not require a click for the asset to be useful.
* Do not publish claims that outrun MOSAIC's evidence.
* Do not optimise for traffic or follower count as the primary outcome.
* Do not launch a generic newsletter unless a recurring promise has already been validated.
* Use AI only to stress-test clarity, evidence, counterarguments and examples; the final judgement and voice remain human.
* No paid media in this experiment.

## Verification

* The content brief names the internal client, job, audience, source evidence and success signal.
* Every asset is understandable without visiting the website.
* The ratio of value deposits to direct asks is recorded.
* Results include all four measurement layers, qualitative evidence and limitations.
* A final decision is recorded: stop, revise, repeat, or scale.
* Scaling requires repeated qualified engagement or a meaningful downstream action—not reach alone.
