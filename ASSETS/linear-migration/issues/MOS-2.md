## Outcome

Review [MOSAIC Mandala — strategic map and next-phase hypothesis](<https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/document/mosaic-mandala-strategic-map-and-next-phase-hypothesis-d31c6b339c7b>) and explicitly choose no more than one to three outcomes for the next phase, or decide that no new work should be committed yet.

## Why

The Mandala exposes possible imbalance across methodology, evidence, reasoning, product, demonstrated value, trust, reach, and adoption. Its value comes from helping choose leverage, not from turning 64 cells into tasks.

## Scope

* Validate or revise the centre and eight conditions for success.
* Identify where MOSAIC is currently most unbalanced.
* Evaluate the eight high-leverage candidates.
* Decide whether the next phase should emphasise formalising, demonstrating, or putting MOSAIC in people's hands.
* Decide whether selected work belongs in an existing project or warrants a new project.
* Create implementation issues only after those outcome decisions are explicit.

## Constraints

* The 64 cells are a strategic map, not a backlog.
* Select no more than one to three outcomes.
* Do not create work merely because a cell is interesting.
* Preserve the existing active proposition project and its current commitments unless a deliberate decision changes them.

## Verification

* The review decision is recorded in the Mandala document or a linked planning note.
* Selected outcomes, if any, have a clear project home.
* Non-selected ideas remain uncommitted strategic context.
