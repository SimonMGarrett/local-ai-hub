# MOS-8 — Audit MOSAIC’s zero-click discovery surfaces using reader language

- Source: https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-8/audit-mosaics-zero-click-discovery-surfaces-using-reader-language
- Status: Backlog
- Project: none
- Updated: 2026-09-24T09:01:49.648Z

## Outcome

Create an evidence-based baseline of how a relevant person currently discovers and understands MOSAIC across search, AI answers, social/community surfaces and the MOSAIC website.

## Why

The supplied zero-click marketing talk argues that first impressions increasingly occur before a website visit. MOSAIC should know what those first impressions are before investing in content or optimisation.

This work is intentionally gated by <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue>. The audit should use the words, questions and misunderstandings observed from real first-time readers rather than invented marketing keywords.

## Scope

* Extract approximately ten high-intent questions from <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue> evidence, covering the problem, desired outcome, method, comparison, trust and first useful action.
* Select only the discovery surfaces plausibly used by the people represented in <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue>.
* For each question and surface, record:
  * whether MOSAIC appears;
  * the answer or impression formed without a click;
  * sources cited, quoted or repeated;
  * inaccurate, missing or unsupported claims;
  * the apparent format and community norms;
  * a responsible proof point MOSAIC could publish, if any.
* Distinguish owned, earned and third-party surfaces.
* Identify no more than two channels suitable for a bounded pilot.
* Add findings and recommendations to the zero-click strategy document.

## Constraints

* Research only; do not post, contact communities, solicit reviews or change public copy.
* Do not treat the talk's quantitative claims as verified evidence.
* Do not use generic search-volume lists as a substitute for reader language.
* Do not recommend every platform.
* Do not optimise for AI mentions at the expense of accuracy.
* Preserve screenshots or dated notes where results are volatile.

## Verification

* Ten questions are traceable to observed reader evidence or explicitly marked as hypotheses.
* Each audited surface has a dated observation and source.
* The output states where MOSAIC is absent, misunderstood or unsupported.
* The recommendation selects zero, one or two pilot channels with a clear rationale.
* The audit identifies one validated worked example or proof unit suitable for standalone use, or explicitly concludes that none is ready.
