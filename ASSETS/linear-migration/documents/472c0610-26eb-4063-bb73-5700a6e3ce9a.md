## Executive assessment

The talk reinforces a useful shift for MOSAIC:

> Discovery is no longer a reliable journey from a link to a website. MOSAIC must be understandable, credible and memorable on the surfaces where a prospective user first encounters it.

For MOSAIC, this does **not** mean publishing more generic content. It means turning the product’s strongest reasoning patterns, examples and proof into self-contained public evidence that is useful without a click.

The immediate constraint remains unchanged: MOSAIC has not yet completed its first reader-comprehension test or validated its most useful first interaction. Marketing scale before that evidence would amplify an unclear proposition.

Recommended sequence:

1. **Validate the proposition and first useful interaction** through <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue>.
2. **Audit the public discovery surfaces** using the language real readers use.
3. **Run one bounded zero-click pilot** around one validated worked example.
4. **Measure patterns between attention, intent and meaningful action**, not last-click attribution.
5. **Scale only if the pilot creates useful learning or qualified demand.**

## What changes for MOSAIC

### 1. The website is not the only homepage

A prospective user may form their first impression from:

* a search result or snippet;
* an AI-generated answer;
* a founder post;
* a specialist discussion;
* a talk, newsletter or podcast mention;
* a colleague’s recommendation;
* a worked example quoted elsewhere.

The product proposition must therefore survive outside the full website. A short description, worked insight or proof point should still communicate:

* the decision situation;
* why ordinary analysis is insufficient;
* what MOSAIC helps a person do;
* what tangible result remains;
* what MOSAIC does **not** automate or decide.

### 2. The atomic marketing unit should be a useful decision insight

A MOSAIC content unit should deliver standalone value, for example:

* one way to distinguish evidence from inference;
* one example of source dependence changing an analysis;
* one decision-changing unknown;
* one reconsideration trigger;
* one before-and-after decision frame;
* one short decision record showing why an action was reasonable at the time.

These units can be repurposed into a founder post, company archive entry, email note, talk example and fuller website page. The reusable asset is the underlying insight and evidence, not a platform-specific post.

### 3. Public evidence is part of product trust

MOSAIC’s differentiator is defensible reasoning. Important proof should not remain only in private notes or internal discussions. When evidence supports it, MOSAIC should publish:

* transparent worked decisions;
* method definitions;
* traceable sources and assumptions;
* explicit limitations;
* anonymised case evidence;
* revision history or reconsideration conditions.

This helps people and answer engines encounter a more accurate public record. It must not become an attempt to manipulate forums, reviews or AI systems.

### 4. Content should have an internal client and a job

Before creating an asset, record:

 1. Internal client: product research, adoption, user education, trust, sales or another named function.
 2. Job to be done.
 3. Audience and decision context.
 4. Desired asset or native format.
 5. Moment of use.
 6. Desired next action.
 7. Source material and subject-matter owner.
 8. Success signal and target.
 9. Distribution plan.
10. Shelf life and refresh trigger.

If these cannot be answered, the asset is not ready.

Examples:

| Asset | Internal client | Job | Useful success signal |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Worked decision | Product research / adoption | Demonstrate the method and reveal misunderstanding | Readers can explain and transfer the principle |
| Case study | Adoption / sales | Reduce perceived risk with credible proof | Qualified conversations, shorter decision cycle |
| Method note | Trust | Make a claim citable and inspectable | Relevant mentions, references, informed questions |
| How-to guide | User education | Help someone apply one MOSAIC move | Completion, reuse, reduced clarification |
| Founder post | Discovery | Teach one useful idea in the feed | Qualified engagement, branded discovery, subscriber growth |

### 5. Measurement should distinguish attention, interest and outcomes

Traffic is a diagnostic, not the primary success measure.

Use four layers:

| Layer | MOSAIC examples |
| -- | -- |
| Audience | Relevant followers, repeat readers, subscribers, direct participants |
| Reach | Qualified impressions, views, minutes watched or read |
| Interest | Saves, thoughtful replies, shares, branded search, worked-example use |
| Outcome | Reader-test participation, useful feedback, qualified conversations, repeat use, pilot or adoption |

The primary question is not “Which post got the conversion?” It is:

> When MOSAIC increases useful exposure among a defined audience, do meaningful actions and evidence of understanding rise afterwards?

Use a simple monthly correlation view. Retain self-reported discovery (“How did you first hear about MOSAIC?”) when a meaningful action exists. Where volume is sufficient, use bounded channel or timing tests rather than trusting platform attribution.

### 6. Own one durable relationship, but only when the exchange is useful

Email is a plausible owned channel because it preserves a direct relationship. MOSAIC should not add a generic newsletter merely to collect addresses.

A subscription becomes justified when it offers a clear recurring benefit, such as:

* one worked decision pattern;
* one decision-quality diagnostic;
* one annotated case;
* one method improvement or evidence update.

The value exchange must be explicit and proportionate.

### 7. Founder voice and company record have different roles

A working hypothesis from the talk:

* **Founder channel:** perspective, judgement, stories and lessons.
* **Company channel:** stable record of examples, methods, releases and evidence.

The founder voice may earn attention; the company record should preserve durable, citable material. Neither should merely repeat the other.

### 8. AI should stress-test content, not replace MOSAIC’s voice or evidence

Use AI to challenge a finished draft:

* Where is the claim weak?
* Which example is missing?
* What could be misunderstood?
* Which counterargument is strongest?
* What evidence would change the conclusion?

Do not use AI to manufacture generic thought leadership, invented proof or an authoritative voice unsupported by MOSAIC’s work.

## Proposed operating model

### Now: complete validation

* Complete <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue> without changing the proposition between initial participants.
* Capture the phrases people naturally use for the problem, desired outcome and tangible result.
* Identify recurring questions, misconceptions and transfer failures.
* Decide which worked example and first action are genuinely useful.

### Next: discovery audit

Using the validated reader language, define approximately ten high-intent questions and inspect the surfaces where a relevant audience may encounter answers.

Record:

* whether MOSAIC appears;
* what answer or impression is currently formed;
* which sources are cited or repeated;
* which claims are inaccurate, absent or unsupported;
* which communities and formats appear genuinely relevant;
* which proof MOSAIC could responsibly publish.

This is research, not reputation management.

### Then: four-week pilot

Choose no more than two channels on evidence, not preference. Publish one or two self-contained assets per week from a single validated worked example.

Suggested constraints:

* one underlying idea per asset;
* native value without requiring a click;
* roughly five value deposits for each direct ask;
* founder and company roles kept distinct;
* one clear, proportionate action when an ask is made;
* every claim traceable to source material;
* no synthetic engagement, forum seeding or mass AI copy.

Review weekly for learning and monthly for patterns. Change channel choice only after the pilot unless evidence shows clear harm or irrelevance.

## Development implications

Do not build a content platform from this talk. Development work becomes justified only where it strengthens the validated experience or makes trustworthy evidence reusable.

Potential later requirements:

* stable URLs for worked decisions and method claims;
* shareable summaries that remain intelligible out of context;
* visible provenance, assumptions, limitations and revision dates;
* structured metadata for accurate search snippets and previews;
* lightweight, privacy-respecting capture of meaningful actions and self-reported discovery;
* reusable representations of a decision insight across website, email and native posts.

These remain hypotheses until <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue> and the discovery audit identify a real need.

## Success gates

Proceed from audit to pilot only if:

* <issue id="43046464-d06a-4ba0-ad94-cef849fc3233" href="https://linear.app/sheridanandbairns/issue/MOS-5/run-the-first-mosaic-reader-comprehension-test">MOS-5</issue> produces a proposition and first interaction that readers can explain;
* at least one worked example teaches a principle accurately;
* the audit finds a plausible audience/surface match;
* MOSAIC has a useful standalone insight with source support;
* a meaningful next action exists.

Scale the pilot only if it produces at least one of:

* repeated qualified engagement from the intended audience;
* clearer branded discovery or direct recall;
* subscriber growth tied to a useful recurring promise;
* reader-test or research participation;
* qualified conversations;
* evidence that people can explain or apply a MOSAIC principle.

## Guardrails

* Do not treat traffic, impressions or follower growth as the primary outcome.
* Do not publish on every platform.
* Do not spam Reddit or other communities.
* Do not optimise for answer-engine mentions at the expense of truth.
* Do not create a generic newsletter before a useful recurring promise exists.
* Do not let AI author MOSAIC’s judgement, evidence or voice.
* Do not infer causality from one attribution report or one high-performing post.
* Do not let marketing claims outrun product evidence.
* Do not create a broad launch while the proposition and first experience remain unvalidated.
